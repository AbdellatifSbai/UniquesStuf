“Publication Etsy + Pinterest” (the “Application”) is a private desktop tool operated by [Your name / shop name] (“we”, “us”). It is used only by its owner to publish the owner's own digital products to the owner's own Etsy shop and Pinterest account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

1. Information the Application accesses

When the owner authorizes the Application through Etsy's and Pinterest's official OAuth sign-in, it can access:

  • Etsy: the shop identifier and the shop's listings (to create listings, upload listing images and digital files, and activate listings). Permissions requested: listings_r, listings_w, shops_r.
  • Pinterest: the list of the owner's boards and pins (to create Pins that link to the owner's Etsy listings). Permissions requested: boards:read, pins:read, pins:write, user_accounts:read.
  • Access tokens issued by Etsy and Pinterest, so the Application can act on the owner's behalf.

The Application does not access buyers' personal data, orders, messages, payment information, or any data belonging to other Etsy or Pinterest users.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to publish the owner's products to Etsy and to create matching Pins on Pinterest. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

  • All data (API keys, access tokens, publication log) is stored locally on the owner's computer, in files that are not shared.
  • The Application has no server and no database; nothing is sent anywhere other than the official Etsy and Pinterest APIs, over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • No cookies, trackers or analytics tools are used.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Data is exchanged only with Etsy and Pinterest, as needed to perform the actions the owner requests.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Etsy: Account settings → Apps (or Security) → remove the Application.
  • Pinterest: Settings → Security and logins (or Apps) → revoke access.

Deleting the local files tokens.json and journal_publications.json permanently removes all stored data. Content published to Etsy or Pinterest can be deleted directly on those platforms.

6. Children

The Application is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.

This policy may be updated; the effective date above will change accordingly.
